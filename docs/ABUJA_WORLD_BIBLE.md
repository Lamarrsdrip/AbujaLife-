# Abuja world bible

## Visual thesis

AbujaLife should be recognizable through **urban form**, not landmarks pasted everywhere.

Common language:
- unusually broad roads and medians
- planned setbacks
- green verges and mature vegetation
- rocky/hilly horizons in selected views
- modern villas beside institutional/commercial architecture
- strong wet-season greens; dusty/hazy harmattan treatment in dry season
- clean daytime civic areas contrasted with denser nightlife/commercial pockets

## District differentiation

### Wuse 2
High social density. Mixed-use frontage, eateries, lounges, retail, offices, ride activity, night lighting, more pedestrians and curb activity.

### Jabi
Water-oriented leisure. Modern apartments/hospitality/retail, lake promenade, dates, fitness, boating-inspired ambience where appropriate.

### Maitama
Low density, expensive homes, walls/gates, mature trees, quiet roads, security/driveways, premium property gameplay.

### Asokoro
Elevation, prestige compounds, greener slopes, formal properties and quieter roads.

### Garki
Older planned fabric, markets/services/offices/residential mix, more everyday errands and transport activity.

### Gwarinpa
Huge residential lifestyle loop: estates, local retail, schools/services, house progression, friend visits.

### Central Area
Large-scale formal boulevards and civic/corporate silhouettes. Career, events, tourism and prestige missions.

### Airport Corridor
Speed, logistics, hotels, new development, roadside businesses and travel loop.

## Scale

Do not model the whole FCT 1:1. Use a compressed open-world topology: each district is a high-fidelity playable cluster connected by believable arterial travel. The player experiences a large city without requiring console-scale storage.
