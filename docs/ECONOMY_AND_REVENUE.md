# Economy and revenue

## Abuja Naira
Virtual, closed-loop game currency. It has no cash value and is not withdrawable.

Players earn it through gameplay and may buy packs through the appropriate platform payment system.

## Revenue surfaces
- currency packs
- cosmetic fashion/home/vehicle customization
- optional premium season/event content later
- tasteful virtual placements/sponsorships later
- Okrika acquisition value from physical marketplace traffic

Never sell raw competitive power that makes free players irrelevant.

## Ledger invariants
- server is balance authority
- all changes create immutable ledger entries
- purchases are idempotent
- payment receipts are verified server-to-server before grants
- no client balance mutation endpoint
- real Okrika items cannot be purchased with Abuja Naira
