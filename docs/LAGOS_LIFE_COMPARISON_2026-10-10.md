# AbujaLife compared with Lagos Life (studied 10 October 2026)

Studied read-only through the owner's signed-in session: Home, Sim panel (Needs, Goals, Skills, People, Career),
Today's Hustle, Buy mode, the Map with its Lagos and Abuja views, and the Billboards list. The Phone apps were not
read. Nothing was bought, posted or changed. No code, art or text was copied; this is a feature comparison.

## Where they were ahead, and what we did about it

| Area | Lagos Life | AbujaLife before | AbujaLife now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Map look | Bright, dense toy city: wide roads, roundabouts, forests, big rocks, ground names | Empty olive slab, hair-thin roads, tiny cluster | Dense housing by district, forests, roundabouts, wider roads, Aso and Zuma rocks, ground names; opens framed on the city |
| Billboards | ~25 named digital boards on real roads, each rotating many ads every 15 s; MEGA tier; exclusive airport boards | One flat ground tile per advertiser, invisible in the 3D street | Every placement stands up as a billboard on the Map; street scene has rotating digital boards showing live campaigns |
| Daily loop | Today's Hustle: 3 missions, city Rep level with titles, 7-day check-in | None | Today's Hustle with Abuja Rep and check-in streak, fed by real actions |
| Things to do | Missions tied to places, story missions | Quiz-style shifts only | Street gigs: rides, deliveries, errands, physically carried out and paid by the server |
| Street life | n/a (their play view is a room diorama plus the map) | Sparse street | Third-person 3D street with district-specific buildings, taxis, keke, lights, kiosks |

## Where they are still ahead (not built yet)

1. **Billboard business model.** Theirs sells airtime on a board (many advertisers per board, from ₦250,000 for 7 days,
   MEGA and exclusive tiers). Ours still sells one placement to one advertiser at ₦2,000 for 7 days. Our street boards now
   rotate every live campaign, but the *sales* model is unchanged. Moving to airtime pricing is a pricing decision.
2. **Story missions** ("Lagos Stories": branching, place-based, with Rep rewards).
3. **Skills** (nine, with slow decay) and a **career ladder** with rank requirements (shifts, skill level, Rep level).
4. **Feelings and traits**: moodlets with named causes ("Fine House +18", "Starving −30"), health states that send you
   to the hospital.
5. **Buy mode depth**: 11 categories including pets and skill objects, star quality, colour variants.
6. **Economy scale**: realistic Naira (₦500 chair, ₦21,000 shift). Ours is inflated (₦10M start, ₦250k–750k shifts).
   Rebalancing would touch every price and existing wallets.
7. **Place icons**: emoji bubbles for every venue on the Map; ours are still plain text pills.
8. **Daily collectible hunt** and house-invite links.

## Where we are ahead

- A real third-person 3D street you walk and drive in; theirs is a room view plus a map.
- Driving gameplay with paid gigs.
- District-specific city fabric and Abuja traffic (green city taxis, keke outside the centre).
