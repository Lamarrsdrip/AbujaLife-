# Build roadmap

## M0 — foundation (this repository)
World streaming, movement, interactions, vehicle/pedestrian primitives, authoritative economy/jobs/market/social domain, Abuja data and platform architecture.

## M1 — playable Wuse 2 vertical slice
One high-quality exterior block, starter apartment, original avatar, one car, one job, City Market, wallet sandbox, second-player presence.

## M2 — Jabi social loop
Lakefront/social venue, group invites, ride system, dining, events, better NPC schedules.

## M3 — housing + property
Gwarinpa/Maitama property progression, furniture placement, visitors, permissions, save/load.

## M4 — business + Okrika growth
Player businesses, advertising inventory, Okrika context handoffs and attribution.

## M5 — launch hardening
Moderation/admin, load tests, crash telemetry, store billing, account recovery, privacy controls, WebGL memory/streaming work, App Store/Play review.
